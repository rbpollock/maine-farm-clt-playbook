# Maine Farm Community Land Trust Playbook

A practical, open-source guide for Maine farms exploring community ownership through a Community Land Trust (CLT) + cooperative structure.

## What this is

This playbook helps farm owners, operators, and community members navigate the transition from private ownership to durable community stewardship. It is designed specifically for Maine's legal, financial, and agricultural landscape.

The model:
- **Land** is held permanently by a community land trust or mission-aligned nonprofit, removing it from speculation
- **Operations** are run by a multi-stakeholder cooperative under a long-term ground lease
- **Housing and auxiliary structures** are documented through CLT leases or small housing cooperatives

## Who this is for

- Farm owners considering a legacy-preserving sale
- Farm operators and workers seeking secure tenure + democratic voice
- Community members who want local food, culture, and gathering spaces to endure
- Facilitators, lawyers, and funders supporting farm transitions

## How to use this playbook

1. Start with [01 - Overview & Principles](docs/01-overview-and-principles.md) to understand the model
2. Use [02 - Funder Brief](docs/02-funder-brief.md) when approaching lenders, foundations, or CDFIs
3. Follow [03 - Transition Playbook](docs/03-transition-playbook.md) as your step-by-step roadmap
4. Share [04 - Member FAQ](docs/04-member-faq.md) with community members
5. Reference governance, legal, and capital sections as you move through each phase

## Maine-specific resources

This playbook references organizations and structures relevant to Maine:

- **[Cooperative Development Institute (CDI)](https://cdi.coop)** — Technical assistance for cooperative formation
- **[Cooperative Fund of New England (CFNE)](https://cooperativefund.org)** — CDFI lending to cooperatives
- **[Coastal Enterprises, Inc. (CEI)](https://ceimaine.org)** — CDFI with farm and food systems focus
- **[Genesis Community Loan Fund](https://genesisloans.org)** — CDFI serving northern New England
- **[Community Building and Mentoring Alliance (CMBA)](https://cmballiance.org)** — Community wealth building
- **[Land in Common](https://landincommon.org)** — Community land trust work in Maine
- **[Maine Farmland Trust](https://mainefarmlandtrust.org)** — Farmland protection and access
